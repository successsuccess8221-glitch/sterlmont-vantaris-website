01 · Company

A focused commercial and investment role

Sterlmont Vantaris LLC is established as a Nevis limited liability company. Its activities comprise two distinct functions: principal-to-principal trading transactions and an equity-holding function.

For its trading activity, the Company will source and purchase goods from independent suppliers in the United Arab Emirates, China and Türkiye and resell those goods to ЧДММ «Маркази рушди саноатӣ» (MRS) under a supply agreement. MRS is the identified customer and intended final recipient under the business model described.

The Company has not identified any other service lines, product categories or revenue-generating activities for the proposed account at this stage.

02 · Commercial activities

What the Company does

The commercial offering is centred on procuring and reselling goods required by MRS for its industrial development project.

01

Supplier sourcing & procurement

Identifying and engaging independent suppliers in the United Arab Emirates, China and Türkiye for goods required by MRS.

02

Purchase & resale contracting

Entering into purchase arrangements with suppliers and corresponding supply arrangements with MRS, with transaction terms documented in written contracts.

03

Commercial coordination

Coordinating order requirements, delivery arrangements and supporting commercial documentation among suppliers, logistics providers and MRS.

04

Payment & settlement administration

Arranging supplier settlement by telegraphic transfer or documentary letter of credit and receiving customer payments against supply agreements and commercial invoices.

05

Equity participation

Holding the Company’s 30% participation in MRS and receiving any distributions attributable to that participation.

06

Transaction records

Maintaining records of supplier purchases, corresponding resale arrangements, settlements and supporting transaction documentation.

03 · Trading model

Principal-to-principal transactions

For each transaction, the Company contracts with the supplier, agrees purchase terms, enters into the corresponding resale arrangement with MRS, coordinates settlement and maintains transaction records.

01 · SourceIdentify independent suppliers and agree purchase requirements and terms.
02 · ContractEnter into the supplier purchase contract and corresponding resale arrangement with MRS.
03 · CoordinateCoordinate shipment, delivery arrangements and supporting commercial documents.
04 · Settle & recordArrange settlement, receive customer payment and maintain transaction records.

Direct shipment

The Company does not maintain physical inventory in Nevis. Goods are intended to be shipped directly from the relevant supplier to the delivery point in Tajikistan.

Commercial role

The Company’s role in the supply chain is contractual, commercial and financial rather than a local warehousing or physical distribution operation in Nevis.
